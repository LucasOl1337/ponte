Settings

Pointer

Direct: the device's pointer goes exactly where you point. The usual way to work.

Locked: the pointer disappears from this PC and raw movement goes out, as in a game or 3D app. Esc gives the pointer back.

Keyboard and shortcuts

Ctrl+X
Switches the keyboard and mouse between this device and the one on the screen. Works with or without full screen.
Hold Esc 2 s
In full screen, leaves and comes back to this device.
Click the screen
Starts controlling.

Ctrl+X is reserved for switching, not cutting in this window. Ctrl+Shift+X does not switch.

In the window, letters, numbers and Ctrl+C/V go to the device, but Super (the Omarchy shortcuts) and the browser's own shortcuts (Ctrl+T, Ctrl+W) stay on this PC: the browser only hands those keys over in full screen. In full screen everything goes.
